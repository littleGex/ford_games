# Catch the Elf Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** An evening hide-and-seek mission for P and Dude: in the dark house the elf hops between hiding spots until 20:30; spot him for stars; stand where he lands and he freezes and hops on.

**Architecture:** A pool of evening spots and a hop picker (`pack/elfspots.js`); `MISSIONS.elfCatch` runs the hops on the mission clock in `update`, with a "Spot him!" job re-made at each landing; `systems/elf.js` places, freezes and hides him; `systems/bedtime.js` lends its night lighting; the best keeps a sightings count; a home card offers it on the report's days.

**Tech Stack:** plain ES modules, three.js, Node play-through tests, Playwright.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-15-elf-catch-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- Clock 20:00 to 20:30 (`clock: { start: hm(20), end: hm(20.5), secondsPerMinute: 6 }`, `endsAtClock: true`); hops keep coming until it runs out.
- Stay ~9 real s at a spot on the same floor as the last, ~14 s after a change of floor; ~1 s hidden between; about one hop in three to another floor; never the same spot twice running.
- Stars: ≥3 sightings ★★★, 2 ★★, otherwise ★. Titles "Elf spotter!" / "Nearly had him!" / "He's a quick one!"; stats "<n> sightings" ("1 sighting").
- Freeze: player within `REACH` (config.js, 1.0) of a spot when he lands there → he freezes ~1 s, no sighting possible, then hops on; toast "He saw you! He's gone stiff as a toy…".
- Hint at each landing: toast and job status naming floor and room; nothing about the next spot.
- Never use a spot within 1 m (same level) of tomorrow's scene elf place (`pickScene(tomorrow, ELF_SCENES, isOn)`); no scene mess props shown during the go.
- Best: `{ stars, minutes, count }`; at equal stars more sightings wins; shown "★★★ 5 spotted" (home and results).
- Offered on the report's days (`reportOffered` in ui/home.js: day after arrival to 23 Dec, Christmas on, not left); P and Dude.
- Names via `nameOf`; text via `textContent`. Every change under `house/` → `CACHE_NAME` bump, new files listed (`tests/house/sw.mjs`). Commits end `Co-Authored-By: Claude Opus 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com>`; restore `test-results/.last-run.json` before committing.

## Review Focus

- Today's elf not found yet, a go played, then back to the day: he's back in his scene spot with his mess, "Find the elf" undone, daylight back.
- The go starting with the player already at the first spot: he freezes, not a free sighting.
- Pressing "Spot him!" just as he hops (stay over): no sighting for an elf who's gone; no double count for one stop.
- The home menu open mid-go (clock paused): he doesn't keep hopping behind the menu.
- Dragon group off: no spot that needs the terrarium; tomorrow's scene changes with the dragon setting, and the exclusion follows it.
